Chapter 6

Assembly

Seven short sections, in this order: the body (tub), the lid, the head, the four legs, the tail, the ears, and closing it all up. The body, lid and head are built as separate pieces; the legs go on the body, and the lid with the head closes it at the end.

Before you start each section, count its screws into a cup (3.3), lay out its parts, and read all its steps once.

Every servo must be centred before its horn goes on (5.4). If you turned a shaft by hand since then, run the servo sketch again first.

Keep the cells out of the robot for the whole of this chapter. They go in during chapter 7.

ABody

The tub holds the four hip servos, the two servo boards, the protection board and fuse, the charger, the power switch and five of the seven lasers. The battery door carries the cell holder. The side panels close the sides.

{{steps:A}}

BLid

The lid is built on its own, upside down on the table. It carries the motion sensor, the touch board, the power hub, the two side lasers, the tail servo and four touch pads, and the collar screws on top of it.

{{steps:B}}

CHead

The head holds the screen, the microphones, the speaker, the camera and the gesture sensor. It is built on the table, then screwed onto the collar on the lid. The head back stays off until the very end.

Note

The muzzle (step C5) may change in the next version of the design. The rest of the head stays the same.
{{steps:C}}

DLegs

Each leg is a thigh and a shin, each made of an outer half and a cover, plus a wheel. It carries the knee servo and the wheel servo, and hangs on the hip servo in the tub. All the leads run inside the leg. Build one leg all the way, then the other three.

{{steps:D}}

ETail

The tail is a root that turns in the socket on top of the lid, with the tail keyed onto it.

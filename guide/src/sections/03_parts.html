A USB-C data cable. Test yours: plug your phone into the laptop with it. If the phone shows up as a device, the cable can program the boards.

Wheel tyres

The tyres come from the AliExpress NBR O-ring assortment (outside diameter 21 to 35 mm, all 2.4 mm thick). Take out four rings of the 31 mm outside diameter size, plus a spare: each is about 26.2 mm across the inside, which is what the wheel groove is designed for. Check the thickness with the caliper (2.4 mm), and try one on a wheel (or the groove on the Fit_Test_Coupon) before you fit all four.

3.2Check the listings match

These look-alike parts cost less and are easy to get wrong. Check each one against the part in your hand.

Screen board: the variant must be "C", which means capacitive touch (the GT911 chip). The "N" and "R" variants look the same.

2S charging board: it must be the "boost" type with 5 V input. A look-alike "buck" board (TP5100 type) cannot charge from USB.

Battery holder: the SERIES version, with one red and one black lead and the two cells end to end in series.

Microphones (MS3625): each must have an I2S interface and an L/R pin.

Speaker: the "1.25 terminal" variant, so it plugs straight into the screen board.

Servos: ten 180 degree MG90S and four 360 degree MG90S. The 360 ones keep turning when you push the pulse off centre; flag them "360" the day they arrive, because they look the same.

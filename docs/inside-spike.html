Inside Spike: take the robot dog apart, piece by piece

Explore it yourself

Take him apart your way.

Pull him apart with a slider, build him one step at a time the way it happens on the bench, or follow the power from the wall socket to every part that moves, thinks and speaks.

This is Spike v3.4g, exported straight from the CAD in his standing pose, so every part sits in its real place. The electronics are the CAD's own simple models, so most boards show as plain blocks. Screws, servo horns and servo leads are left out. He has not been printed yet: this is the design, not a photo.

The 3D parts (about 2.6 MB) load once for the whole page. Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap a part to see what it does.

Before you start

Two minutes. Then the box.

0.1

Have these ready.

Scissors for one zip-tie, dish soap, paper towel, your phone, your laptop and your Wi-Fi password. The Allen keys are in the box.

0.2

Every print is the same 8 steps.

You will learn them on Plate 0. After that it is just the same 8 steps again.

Put the printer on a flat, steady table.

6

Clean the plate

Do this before the first print.

6.1

Wash the Textured PEI plate.

Wet it. Dish soap. Scrub with a sponge or a plastic brush. Rinse with warm water. Dry with a paper towel.

CarefulHold the plate by the sides. Fingers leave grease on the top.
Follows: Bambu wiki: Textured PEI Plate clean guide
6.2

Never use acetone. Never use soap with oils or moisturiser.

Bambu: acetone damages the PEI. Oils and moisturisers stay on the plate and stop the first layer sticking.

Follows: Bambu wiki: Textured PEI Plate clean guide
6.3

Test it: rinse the plate. Water should spread as a thin film.

Water that runs into beads or droplets means the plate is still greasy. Wash it again.

Follows: Bambu wiki: Textured PEI Plate clean guide

Left side: Filament must match the roll you loaded.

The projects say Bambu PLA Matte. Loaded a Bambu PLA Basic roll instead? Pick Bambu PLA Basic. The free 20 g sample: pick Bambu PLA Basic (see the check).

Tap Slice plate.

Wait a few seconds. Do not change any other setting. The file already holds Spike's settings: 0.20 mm layers, supports only where a part needs them, brims where the CAD asks for them.

CheckEvery plate was sliced by Bambu Studio itself on 17 Oct 2026 with these files; the times and grams on the plate cards are from that slice.

Look at Preview. Slide the layer bar up and down.

Plate 0 should say about 8 minutes and about 2 g. Every part must sit inside the bed.

ExpectNo part hangs over the edge. Nothing floats in the air.

Tap Print plate. Keep Bed Leveling and Flow Dynamics Calibration ticked. Then send it.

Bambu recommends both. The printer heats, levels itself, then prints. Timelapse is up to you. Use AMS: leave it off unless you have the AMS lite.

Stay for the first layer.

About 5 to 10 minutes. You can also watch on the camera in Bambu Handy.

Look at the first layer.

Flat lines joined to each other. Round lines with gaps: nozzle too high. Wavy or peeling lines: nozzle too low.

ExpectGood: smooth. If it is bad, tap Stop and go to section 9.

Wait until the print has finished and the bed is cool.

Bambu: let the heatbed cool down before you take the parts off.

Lift the plate off the heatbed by its sides.

It is held on by magnets.

Bend the plate a little. The parts come off.

The scraper blade from the box is only for stubborn parts. Do not pry hard on the plate.

7.13

The click test: push each cup into its test square. Turn it one sixth of a turn.

It should CLICK, hold firmly, and come back out with a fingernail in the notch.

ExpectClick. Firm. Fingernail turns it out.

When your magnets arrive: drop one into each cup.

A 12 x 3 magnet in the belly cup, an 8 x 3 magnet in the back cup. Each one sits flat.

7.15

Pass or fail?

Pass: go on with plate C1. Fail (too stiff or too loose): do NOT print the other plates. The click bump is changed in the CAD first. The little diode tray on Plate 0 is for later: when the diode board arrives, it slides in and two small lips hold it.

When something goes wrong

Stop first. Then pick the picture that looks like yours.

!

A tangle of thin filament instead of a part ("spaghetti")

You see: Part came off the plate, or the print is a mess of strings.

  1. Tap Stop on the printer screen (or in Bambu Handy). Do not wait.
  2. Let the nozzle cool. Then clear the mess. Do not pull it off a hot nozzle.
  3. Wash the plate with warm water and dish soap. Bambu says this solves most sticking problems.
  4. In Studio check: Plate type = Textured PEI Plate, Bed Leveling on. Then print again.
  5. Still lifting at the corners? See the next card (warping).
CheckThe wiki page for automatic spaghetti detection is about the X1 camera. I found nothing that says the A1 mini does it today. Watch the first layers yourself.
Follows: Bambu wiki: Spaghetti detection
!

Corners or edges lift off the plate (warping)

You see: The part curls up at a corner. A line may show where it lifted.

  1. Wash the plate (warm water, dish soap). Do not touch the top with fingers.
  2. Check Plate type in Studio = the plate you really use. Wrong type = wrong heat and wrong nozzle height.
  3. Turn Bed Leveling on. Print again.
  4. Add a brim: a flat extra edge round the part. Increase its width if it still lifts.
  5. Raise the bed temperature slightly (Bambu: higher bed heat = better grip; too high can cause bulging at the bottom).
Follows: Bambu wiki: Common print quality problems
!

Thin hairs between the parts (stringing)

You see: Fine strings of plastic between two places on the print.

  1. Damp filament is the first suspect. Keep the roll sealed when it is not printing; dry it if it has been out a long time.
  2. Lower the nozzle temperature a little.
  3. Check the nozzle size in Studio is 0.4 mm like the real nozzle.
  4. Keep parts close together on the plate so the nozzle travels less. (Spike's plates are already laid out close.)
Follows: Bambu wiki: Common print quality problems
!

Nothing comes out of the nozzle, or the filament curls

You see: Loading does not push out a straight string. Printing stops or looks thin.

  1. Heat the nozzle a little above PLA temperature (Bambu's page says 250 degrees C) and press Load. Does a straight string come out?
  2. If not: with the nozzle hot, push the Unclogging Pin Tool (from the box) up and down through the nozzle tip. Then press Load again.
  3. Still not? Bambu has a "cold pull" method with pictures. Open the wiki page below and do it step by step.
  4. Printing too fast can also under-melt the filament. Use the normal speed.
CarefulThe nozzle is hot. Wear an oven glove and keep your face away from the nozzle. Molten plastic can spit.
Follows: Bambu wiki: Hotend unclogging (A1 series)
!

The first layer does not stay on the plate

You see: Lines peel up or are dragged along by the nozzle.

  1. Plate type in Studio must match the plate on the printer (Textured PEI Plate).
  2. Wash the plate with warm water and dish soap. Keep Bed Leveling ticked when you print.
  3. Run Auto Bed Leveling (printer screen: Settings, Maintenance, Calibration) with a clean nozzle tip.
  4. Raise the bed temperature slightly, or add a brim.
Follows: Bambu wiki: First Layer Not Sticking
!

The first layer is squashed or wavy (nozzle too close)

You see: Ridges, a rough sheet, or little or no plastic coming out.

  1. Run bed leveling before printing. Do this every time you change the plate.
  2. Check the hotend is tight and the clip is fastened (Bambu's page shows how). If you are not sure, stop and ask.
CarefulThe deeper fixes on that page need tools and taking the hotend apart. Ask first.
Follows: Bambu wiki: First layer troubleshooting (A1 series)

Not sure? Stop and ask.

Tap Stop. Take a photo of the plate and of the screen. Send it to Nur or Claude before you try again. Bambu's own tip: when you ask for help, a picture or a short video of the problem helps.
